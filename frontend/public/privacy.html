Prudence Wallet

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Who we are

Prudence Wallet is developed by Opeyemi Ayoola. If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact yemcels@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed version of your password. We never store your password in plain text.

Financial information. To power the app's core feature — tracking and categorizing your spending — we collect:

  • Transactions and account details you connect through Plaid, when you link a bank account.
  • Transactions and balance information you connect through PayPal.
  • Transactions you enter manually.
  • The rank you or the app's coaching feature assigns to each transaction (e.g. Important, Necessary, Wasteful), and any rules the coaching feature learns from your answers.

Preferences. Your chosen home currency and other in-app settings.

We do not collect your government ID, precise location, contacts, or device advertising identifiers, and the app does not run any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

How we use your information

  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To display your transactions, categorize your spending, and power the coaching and savings-projection features.
  • To keep your linked accounts (Plaid, PayPal) in sync.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Third-party services

When you link an account, your information also passes through that provider under their own privacy policy:

  • Plaid Inc. — used to securely connect bank accounts. See Plaid's privacy policy.
  • PayPal — used to connect your PayPal wallet. See PayPal's privacy policy.

We only receive the transaction and account data these providers share with us on your behalf; we never receive your bank or PayPal login credentials.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored on our hosting provider's servers and transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Passwords are hashed, never stored as plain text, and sessions use signed, expiring tokens. As with any online service, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your rights and choices

You can review your data at any time within the app. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data — transactions, connections, and learned coaching rules — at any time from the app (Delete account, in the header). This deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you're in the UK or EU, you also have rights under GDPR / UK GDPR to access, correct, or export your data, and to object to or restrict our processing of it — contact us at yemcels@gmail.com to exercise these.

Children's privacy

Prudence Wallet is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: yemcels@gmail.com.
